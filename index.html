Your Mentor

Dr. Avanti Cross-Brown, DNP, PMHNP-BC, CARN-AP, NRCME

Dr. Cross-Brown is the Founder and CEO of Brown Behavioral Health Services, a CMS certified Rural Health Clinic serving Tennessee and Mississippi. She is a board-certified psychiatric mental health nurse practitioner, a certified Addictions Advanced Practice Provider, and a certified medical examiner.

She built a multi-state practice that delivers behavioral health, primary care, addiction treatment, school-based telehealth, correctional health, and mobile clinic services, and has secured state funding across Tennessee and Mississippi as a passionate grant writer. She now shares those same systems with the next generation of providers and entrepreneurs.
